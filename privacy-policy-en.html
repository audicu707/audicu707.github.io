Privacy Policy — Corrupt Football

Effective date: 16 August 2026 · Last updated: 16 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Corrupt Football (also published as Yapıyı Yık, package me.audicu707.swipefootball, the “App”) handles your information. The App is developed by audicu707 (“we”, “us”).

Short version

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics. Everything you do stays on your own device.

Information we collect

We collect none. The App does not ask for, gather, or send any personal or sensitive information — no name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers, or usage tracking.

Data stored on your device

To let you keep playing, the App saves game data locally on your device only using the device’s standard storage. This includes your career progress and statistics, game settings (such as language and sound), and any custom club names you set. This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is permanently removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

No third parties

The App contains no advertising networks, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party services that collect data. It does not require an internet connection to play.

Children

Because the App collects no personal data from anyone, it is suitable for users of all ages and complies with children’s privacy requirements. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at audicu707@gmail.com.
