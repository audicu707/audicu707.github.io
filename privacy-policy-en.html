Privacy Policy — Corrupt Football

Effective date: 16 October 2026 · Last updated: 16 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Corrupt Football (also published as Yapıyı Yık and Fútbol Corrupto, package me.audicu707.swipefootball, the “App”) handles your information. The App is developed by audicu707 (“we”, “us”).

Short version

The App is free to play and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. An optional one-time purchase, Remove Ads, is sold through Google Play. We have no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own: we do not collect, store or sell your personal data. Google, as our advertising and payment provider, processes some data as described below.

Data stored on your device

The App saves game data locally on your device only: your career progress and statistics, game settings (such as language and sound), custom club names, whether ads are removed, and a counter used to pace ads. This data never leaves your device through us, is not accessible to us, and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To show ads, the App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s Advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interaction data (ads shown, taps) and diagnostic/crash data related to ads.

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App shows Google’s consent form before any ads are requested, and ads are personalised only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in Options → Privacy Settings. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ad-free players

If you bought Remove Ads, or you installed the App while it was a paid app (original buyers are treated as owning Remove Ads), the App does not load or show ads and does not start the ad SDK. The consent form may still be shown so that your choice is on record.

Purchases (Google Play)

The Remove Ads purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The App only receives a confirmation from Google Play that you own the product, which it uses on your device to switch ads off and to restore the purchase on reinstall or a new device. If a purchase is refunded or cancelled, Remove Ads is withdrawn. Refunds are handled under Google Play’s policies. Google Play’s handling of your data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Sharing of data

We do not sell or share your personal data. The only third parties involved are Google (AdMob for ads and Google Play for purchases), which process data as described above under their own policies.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your choices

  • Change or withdraw ad consent: Options → Privacy Settings (where available).
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Remove all locally stored game data: uninstall the App or clear its storage.
  • Stop seeing ads entirely: buy Remove Ads in Options.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at audicu707@gmail.com.
